Who is responsible

The data controller is Francesco Flamini, in Italy. Contact: support@lilypadcare.com.

What data and why

Your account

Your email address and password, so you can sign in to your account. The password is stored in scrambled form (hashed): nobody can read it, not even us.

Your profile

Your city, country, units and the settings of your reminders. The position of your city is rounded to about 10 km and is used only to estimate your climate and seasons. We do not use your phone's location.

Your plants

Spaces, plants, photos, waterings, journal entries and notes. Your photos are private: only your account can see them. A copy also stays on your phone.

Emails

We write to you only to confirm your sign-up, to reset your password and to tell you about important changes to this page.

City search and climate

The name of the city you search for goes to Open-Meteo. The rounded position of your city goes to NASA POWER, to estimate your climate. Both also see your phone's IP address, as with any request on the internet. Neither receives your email or anything else about your account.

Error reports

If something goes wrong in the app, Sentry receives the error, the screen where it happened, your phone model and its operating system. It does not receive your email, your account or your IP address.

App updates

For app updates, Expo receives your phone's operating system and a random code that does not identify you.

Why we may use them

This is what the law calls the "legal basis":

  • account, profile, plants, emails, city search and climate: the contract, that is, the service you ask us for;
  • error reports and updates: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and safe.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no profiling, and we never sell or rent your data.

To create an account you need an email, a password and a city. Everything else is up to you.

Outside the European Union

Some of these companies are based outside the European Union. Supabase uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Sentry and Expo use the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the standard contractual clauses. Brevo may pass data to the United States and India with safeguards it describes in its privacy policy. Switzerland, where Open-Meteo is based, has an adequacy decision of the European Commission. NASA POWER is a public service of the United States government with no such agreement: it receives only the rounded position of your city and the request's IP address, and keeps its request logs only for a short time, for security.

How long

  • Your data stays as long as you keep your account.
  • Things you delete in the app are removed for good after 30 days.
  • When you delete your account, everything is deleted at once.
  • Nightly backups are encrypted and deleted after 30 days. They do not contain photos.
  • Error reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.
  • Open-Meteo keeps its request logs for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to get a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, to limit its use, or object to it. Write to support@lilypadcare.com from the address of your account. It is free, and we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority of the country where you live.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages: GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Minimum age

You must be at least 14 years old to use LilyPad.

Changes

When the app changes what data it uses, this page changes too and the date at the top is updated. If a change is important, we will tell you by email before it applies.
