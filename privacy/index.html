Who is responsible

The data controller is Francesco Flamini, in Italy. Contact: support@lilypadcare.com.

What data and why

Your account

Your email address and password, so you can sign in to your account. The password is stored in scrambled form (hashed): nobody can read it, not even us.

Your profile

Your city, country, units and the settings of your reminders. The position of your city is rounded to about 10 km and is used only to estimate your climate and seasons. We do not use your phone's location.

Your plants

Spaces, plants, photos, waterings, journal entries and notes. Your photos are private: other users cannot see them. A copy also stays on your phone.

Technical data

Like any online service, our servers see your phone's IP address when the app connects, and keep it in technical logs for a short time, for security.

Emails

We write to you only to confirm your sign-up, to send you a code to reset your password and to tell you about important changes to this page.

City search and climate

The name of the city you search for goes to Open-Meteo. The rounded position of your city goes to NASA POWER, to estimate your climate. Both also see your phone's IP address, as with any request on the internet. Neither receives your email or anything else about your account.

Error reports

If something goes wrong in the app, Sentry receives a report with the error, the screen where it happened and technical details of the phone (model, operating system, app version). The report does not contain your email or your account, and Sentry is set not to keep your IP address.

App updates

For app updates, Expo receives your phone's operating system, a random code created by the app on your phone (not linked to your account) and, as with any request on the internet, your phone's IP address. Expo keeps the data of update checks as its privacy policy says.

Why we are allowed to use your data

This is what the law calls the "legal basis":

  • account, profile, plants, emails, city search and climate: the contract, that is, the service you ask us for;
  • error reports, app updates, backups and technical logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and safe and in not losing your data;
  • this website's visitor logs: the legitimate interest in keeping the site safe.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no profiling, no automated decisions about you, and we never sell or rent your data.

To create an account you need an email, a password and a city: without them we cannot create your account. Everything else is up to you.

Outside the European Union

Some of these companies are based outside the European Union. Supabase uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Sentry, Expo and GitHub use the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the standard contractual clauses. Brevo may pass data to the United States and India with safeguards it describes in its privacy policy. Switzerland, where Open-Meteo is based, has an adequacy decision of the European Commission. NASA POWER is a public service of the United States government with no such agreement: it receives only the rounded position of your city and the request's IP address, and keeps its request logs only for a short time, for security.

How long

  • Your data stays as long as you keep your account.
  • Things you delete in the app are removed for good after 30 days, and from the backups within 30 more days.
  • When you delete your account, everything is deleted at once, except the copies below, which are deleted on their own.
  • Nightly backups are encrypted, kept by GitHub and deleted after 30 days. They do not contain photos.
  • Technical logs of our servers and email service, which may contain your IP address or email, are kept for up to one month.
  • Error reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.
  • Open-Meteo keeps its request logs for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to get a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how it is used, or to object to its use. Write to support@lilypadcare.com from the address of your account. It is free, and we answer within one month.

Right to object. You can object at any time to the uses based on our legitimate interest (error reports, app updates, backups, technical logs and this website's visitor logs). Write to support@lilypadcare.com: we will stop, unless there are compelling reasons such as the security of the service, which we will explain to you.

You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority of the country where you live.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages: GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. GitHub, Inc. (USA) uses the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and keeps these logs as its privacy statement says.

Minimum age

You must be at least 14 years old to use LilyPad.

Changes

When the app changes what data it uses, this page changes too and the date at the top is updated. If a change is important, we will tell you by email before it applies.
