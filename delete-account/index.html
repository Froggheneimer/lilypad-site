Delete your LilyPad account

Last updated: 21 November 2026

LilyPad: Plant Care is an app by Francesco Flamini. You can delete your account and all your data in two ways.

In the app

You need an internet connection.

  1. At the top of the screen, tap the person icon (Profile & settings)
  2. At the bottom of the page, tap Delete account
  3. When asked “Delete your account?”, tap Delete everything

By email

If you no longer have the app, write from the email address of your account to:

support@lilypadcare.com

We delete the account and answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • At once and for good: your account, profile, spaces, plants, photos, waterings, journal and everything else you entered.
  • The copy on your phone too, when you delete from the app.
  • A few copies stay for a short time and are deleted on their own: the encrypted nightly backups (within 30 days), our providers' technical logs, which may contain your email or IP address (within one month), and error reports, which are not linked to your account (within 90 days).

More about your data in the privacy notice.
